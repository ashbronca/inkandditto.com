##A notes app, first

Ink opens on a fresh page, ready for whatever’s on your mind. Every note is a plain Markdown file, on your iPhone or in iCloud Drive, where your Mac and iPhone stay in step. The page is paper and type and nothing else: headings fold, lists and checklists behave, tables line up, and the marks step into the margin so your words never move.

  • Plain Markdown files you own, in a folder you can open in anything.
  • No AI anywhere until you connect it.
  • Typing never waits on Ditto.

##Hand a line to Ditto

Ditto is for people who build software with Claude. It lives in the margin: when a line is something to do, press ⌘↩.

The empty state on Projects feels like an error

  1. The line you wrote is the brief. Ditto takes it, with the lines around it, and works out what kind of job it is.
  2. Ditto does the work with Claude Code on your Mac: for code, in your project, on a branch of its own; for anything else, a draft for you to copy.
  3. It’s checked before you see it: the tests it can run, then a second look against your taste.
  4. It comes back for one call: Ship It, Ask for Changes or Discard, with what changed and what was checked.

##Say it once

Every call you make, and every reason you give, teaches Ditto your taste. Before you decide, it seals its guess at what you’ll say, and shows you how often it’s right. When it has earned it, it asks whether it may do things like that without asking. Only your yes changes that.

“I expected you’d ship it. You asked for changes — noted: ‘the shadow is still heavy.’”

##Everything you’ve handed over

Ditto’s room shows what needs you first, then what’s being worked on. Pause, change or stop any of it.

##And in your pocket

Ink on iPhone opens on a page, too. Your folders, Ditto and Settings are a swipe away. Make Ditto’s calls in a spare minute, or hand it a line and your Mac starts it.

##Calm, and yours

Your notes stay on your devices and in your iCloud Drive. There’s no account, and nothing comes to us.

When you connect Ditto, it works through Claude Code on your Mac with your own Claude account, and Claude reads what the job needs: the line, the lines around it, and for code, your project. Ditto never pushes your code anywhere or messages anyone.

##Questions

Do I need Ditto to use Ink?

No. Without it, Ink is simply a calm notes app, and Ditto waits quietly until you ask for it.

Why not just use Claude Code?

Ditto runs on Claude Code. It adds what a terminal can’t: the brief is the line you already wrote, the work is checked before you see it, and you answer once. Your note remembers what became of every line.

What do I need for Ditto?

A Mac with Claude Code and a Claude account. On iPhone, Ditto shows and decides the work your Mac is doing.
