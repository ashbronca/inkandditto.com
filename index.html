Plain Markdown files you own.
No account. Nothing comes to us.

Your notes stay on your devices and in your iCloud Drive. There’s no AI anywhere until you connect it. When you do, Ditto works like this.

  • Nothing pushed or merged.

    Ship It commits to Ditto’s branch. What happens next is yours.

  • Asks before it spends.

    If a line isn’t code, Ditto asks first. Stop any work, and nothing more is spent.

  • Your own Claude account.

    Ditto runs Claude Code on your Mac. Your code never passes through us.

It learns your taste.

Every call, and every reason you give, teaches Ditto what you’d ship. The more you use it, the less you have to say.

  • Taste you can read.

    What Ditto believes about your taste is a note. Edit it like any other.

  • A guess, sealed.

    Before you decide, Ditto seals its guess at your call, and shows you how often it was right.

  • Trust, earned.

    Once it beats guessing, it may offer to commit one kind of work without asking. Only if you say yes, and you can switch it off at any time.

Questions.

Why not just use Claude Code?

Ditto runs on Claude Code. It adds what a terminal can’t: the brief is the line you already wrote, the work is checked against your taste before you see it, and you answer once. Your note remembers what became of every line.

Is my code safe?

Yes. It works on its own branch, and never pushes, merges or messages anyone.

What do I need for Ditto?

A Mac with Claude Code, a Claude account and, for code, a project in git. On iPhone, you can hand Ditto a line and decide the work your Mac is doing.

What does it cost?

Ink is free. Ditto is free during early access; Claude Code runs on your own Claude plan.

Can Ditto do more than code?

Today it builds software, can explore a few directions before it builds, and drafts anything else for you to send. Specs, tickets, research summaries and design checks are coming.

Do I need Ditto to use Ink?

No. Ink stands on its own, and Ditto stays out of sight until you connect it.

Who makes it?

Ash Bronca, in Australia. Write to support@inkandditto.com.
